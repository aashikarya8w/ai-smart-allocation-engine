Executive Summary

The Prime Minister’s Internship Scheme (PMIS) is a monumental national program designed to provide 10 million young Indians with structured 12-month internships across India's top 500 corporate organizations. Managing a nationwide allocation pipeline at this magnitude requires uncompromising precision, rigorous affirmative action compliance, multi-dimensional skill evaluation, and zero-leakage fraud prevention.

Central Challenge Solved by the Smart Allocation Engine

Traditional first-come-first-served systems cause metropolitan concentration, neglect aspirational and rural districts, and result in over 38% seat abandonment. Our AI Engine optimizes candidate skill readiness, affirmative action quotas (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Women), and industry capacities simultaneously with mathematical fairness guarantees.

2. Detailed Breakdown of 21 Admin Modules

1. Executive Dashboard (/admin/dashboard)

Central nerve center displaying national KPIs: Total Applicants, Verified Companies, Published Internships, Confirmed Allocations, and National Fairness Score. Features urgent alerts for quota bottlenecks and verification backlogs.

2. AI Smart Matching Studio (/admin/ai-matching)

Interactive matching workshop allowing administrators to fine-tune weighting parameters (Skills: 40%, Geography: 25%, Social Equity: 20%, Assessment: 15%), run live dry-run simulations, and inspect score breakdowns prior to production publish.

3. Digital Identity & Verification Center (/admin/verification)

Automated verification against DigiLocker proofs, QR code credential verification, and Aadhaar-linked status checks. Includes one-click verification approval/rejection with mandatory audit remarks.

4. Affirmative Action & Fairness Index (/admin/fairness)

Real-time visual monitoring of statutory reservation quotas (SC, ST, OBC, EWS), gender diversity indices (>40% women in STEM roles), and geographic representation across 780+ Indian districts using Gini coefficient curves.

5. Seat Matrix & Corporate Capacity (/admin/capacity)

Tracks capacity utilization across 24 industry sectors (IT, Manufacturing, BFSI, Healthcare, Renewable Energy). Flags under-subscribed and over-subscribed programs for administrative quota rebalancing.

6. Dynamic Reallocation Engine (/admin/reallocation)

Detects dropped or unaccepted internship offers and automatically re-ranks waitlisted candidates based on compatibility scores, triggering secondary allocation rounds within seconds.

7. Peer-to-Peer Mutual Swaps (/admin/swaps)

Enables two candidates to request a mutual seat swap if personal displacement constraints arise. The AI verifies compatibility, ensuring company criteria and reservation quotas are not violated, before routing for admin approval.

8. Geo-Tagged Check-in & Monitoring (/admin/check-in)

Monitors daily intern attendance via geo-fenced mobile check-ins. Disputed or off-premise attendance records are flagged for company verification before monthly government stipend release.

9. Forensic Audit Trail (/admin/audit-logs)

Immutable, append-only security log recording every single administrative action: Actor ID, Modified Entity, Before/After Diff, Client IP, and Timestamp for complete transparency and CAG audit readiness.

10. Ministry Compliance Reports (/admin/reports)

One-click generation of official parliamentary and ministerial compliance documentation in PDF, Excel, and CSV formats, categorized by state, sector, and demographic classifications.

3. AI Matching Algorithm & Mathematical Model

The allocation engine computes a global multi-objective optimization pairing candidates $c_i$ to internships $i_j$:

MatchScore(c_i, i_j) = w_skill · S_skill + w_loc · S_loc + w_acad · S_acad + w_equity · Ω_equity - Ψ_penalty

Component Formulations:

  • Semantic Skill Similarity ($S_{\text{skill}}$): Cosine similarity between candidate skill embedding vector $\vec{v}_{c_i}$ and role requirement vector $\vec{v}_{i_j}$:
    S_skill = (v_c · v_i) / (||v_c|| · ||v_i||)
  • Geospatial Proximity ($S_{\text{loc}}$): Exponential decay based on distance $d$ (in km) to prioritize home-state opportunities:
    S_loc = exp(-d / d_0)
  • Social Equity Multiplier ($\Omega_{\text{equity}}$): Boost factor ensuring candidates from aspirational districts and marginalized backgrounds receive fair prioritization:
    Ω_equity = 1.0 + β_category + β_aspirational_district + β_gender_stem
